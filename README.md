Download the Windows Installer:
- ⬇️ [**Download YouTube Downloader LS (MSI Installer)**](https://github.com/lsutorus/ytd-ls/releases/download/v1.0.2/YouTubeDownloaderLS-v1.0.2.msi)

## Features

- **Simple & Fast:** Paste any YouTube link to automatically preview video info and download.
- **Audio & Video Formats:**
  - **Audio Only (MP3):** Default high-quality 320 kbps conversion.
  - **Video (MP4):** Up to 4K / 8K resolution with crisp merged audio.
  - **Lossless & Raw Audio:** M4A, WAV, and FLAC support.
- **Custom Save Location:** Select your preferred download folder and click **Set Default** to remember it permanently.
- **Clip Trimming:** Enter start and end timestamps (e.g. `00:15` to `01:30`) to download only the section you want.
- **Embedded Artwork & Metadata:** Automatically embeds artist, title, and thumbnail cover art directly into output files.
- **Lightweight & Clean:** Self-contained native installer (~26 MB) with automatic one-time background setup.

## Downloading Age-Restricted & Private Videos

YouTube requires an authenticated 18+ account session to download age-restricted or private content. To enable this in YouTube Downloader LS:

1. **Install an Extension:** Install a free, open-source cookie export extension in your browser, such as [Get cookies.txt LOCALLY](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/get-cookiestxt-locally/cclelndahbckbenkjhflpdbgdldlbecc) (Chrome / Edge / Brave) or [cookies.txt](https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/cookies-txt/) (Firefox).
2. **Export Your Cookies:** Open [youtube.com](https://www.youtube.com) while signed into your verified 18+ account. Click the extension in your browser toolbar and click **Export** to save your `cookies.txt` file.
3. **Import into the App:** In YouTube Downloader LS, switch to the **Advanced** tab, click **Import cookies.txt...**, and select your file (you can also click **❓ Guide** in the app for 1-click links).

> **Note:** Your cookies are saved permanently in your local settings, so you only need to perform this one-minute setup once. You can update or remove them at any time with the **✕ Clear** button in the **Advanced** tab.
